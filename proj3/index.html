Programming Project #3

Flow Models

CS180: Intro to Computer Vision and Computational Photography

In Part A of this project, I used a pretrained text-to-image flow model to add noise to images, remove noise from images, generate images from scratch, and edit images. I also made visual anagrams and hybrid images. In the second half of this project, Part B, I trained UNets on MNIST: first a one-step denoiser, then time- and class-conditioned flow matching models that generate digits from noise.

Part A · Exploring the Power of Flow Models

Unless specified, I used seed 180, Euler step size h = 0.02, the text prompt "a high quality photo", and CFG weight w = 7 wherever guidance is on. For Sections 1.1–1.5 I used my own photo of a pink flower.

Part A 1.1 · Constructing Noisy Training Inputs

I sampled one noise tensor and mixed it with the photo at three times, using interpolate(image, noise, t).

Part A 1.2 · Classical Denoising

To set a baseline that uses no learning, I first divided each noisy input by t in order to restore the image's amplitude, then applied a Gaussian blur. I tried σ ∈ {1, 2, 4, 8, 16} and kept the result with the lowest MSE to the clean photo.

Part A 1.3 · One-Step Denoising

A single model call estimates the clean image: x̂_1 = x_t + (1 − t)·v_θ(x_t, t, c).

Part A 1.4 · Visualizing Velocity

For each time: the noisy image, the predicted update (1−t)·v̂, the ground-truth update x_1 − x_t, and the per-pixel RMS error over RGB.

Part A 1.5 · Iterative Denoising with Euler Sampling

25 Euler updates from the t = 0.5 input with h = 0.02.

Both model-based results are much better than the blur. The one-step result is smoother than the original, but the Euler result is a little crisper.

Part A 1.6 · Generating Images from Noise

Starting from pure noise at t = 0, I took 50 Euler steps with the prompt "a high quality photo" and no guidance, using five seeds.

We see that all five came out as portraits of people, but several are broken/twisted in weird ways. For instance, the face at seed 180 is smeared and missing features, the face at seed 183 is also badly deformed while the torso at seed 181 just looks wrong. Seeds 182 and 184 are relatively reasonable.

Part A 2.1 · Classifier-Free Guidance

The same five seeds as in 1.6, with CFG at w = 7.

The images are coherent with guidance, with all five of the images being front-facing head-and-shoulders portraits with clean faces. They are also more alike than the 1.6 samples, with similar framing and plain studio backgrounds. Thus, CFG trades a bit of variety for quality.
